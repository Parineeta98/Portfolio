About

My journey towards data analytics started with chasing questions. My education taught me how to design a fair test, sit with results sometimes unexpected, and explain what I found to someone who may not have been present for any of it. Somewhere between teaching statistics at the University of Amsterdam and helping clients make sense of survey data as a psychologist, I realized the part of the work I loved most was the numbers themselves and what it lets you say with real confidence. That's what pulled me toward data analytics, and it's where I've been building myself towards ever since.

I seek reliability in my work the same way I chase confidence in the story of my data. I am always looking to learn and work with different types of data and hopefully get to read the story your data tells me.
